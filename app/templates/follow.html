Count is off?

The usual suspects

  • A few too many: often an extra stitch in the first or last stitch of the round, or working into the slip-stitch join.
  • A few too few: a skipped increase, or a missed last stitch. In a spiral it's easy to miss the stitch under the marker.
  • Off by one each round: move your marker up every round, into the first stitch.
  • Way off: check you're reading the right round, then compare a repeat, like "(sc, inc)", with what you made.
  • Only 1 off on a toy? Many makers fix it quietly in the next round with an extra inc or dec.

Time for a little stretch

  • Loosen your grip on the hook and roll your shoulders.
  • Stretch your fingers and wrists, and look away from the work for a moment.
  • Check your tension: are the last few rows the same size as the earlier ones?
